QuadPreRecorder Rev 3 - Assembly Notes
======================================

BOARD
  4-layer FR-4, 138.15 x 114.15 mm, 1.6 mm, 1 oz outer + 1 oz inner
  Stackup top -> bottom:
     L1  F_Cu    signal
     L2  In1_Cu  SOLID GROUND PLANE
     L3  In2_Cu  signal + ground pour
     L4  B_Cu    signal

ASSEMBLY
  Two-sided SMT.  180 placements total: 113 top, 67 bottom.
  74 BOM lines: 61 SMD lines, 13 through-hole lines.

  Fine-pitch parts needing care:
     U7   PCM1864DBT   TSSOP-30, 0.5 mm pitch
     U10  TPA6132A2    WQFN-16 with exposed thermal pad, 0.5 mm pitch
     U1   TPS62160DGK  VSSOP-8, 0.65 mm pitch
     U4,U5,U6,U9       TSSOP, 0.65 mm pitch

  Through-hole parts (13): J1 J2 J3 J4 J5 J6 J7 RV1 SW1 SW2 SW3 SW4 U8

  U8 is a socket for a Teensy 4.1 module. Please confirm whether the
  Teensy 4.1 module itself is included in your quote or supplied by me.

SUBSTITUTION POLICY  (see the Substitution column in the BOM)
  This is an audio product. The BOM marks every line either:

    "EXACT PART - do not substitute"   (27 lines)
        All ICs, connectors, switches, diodes, the inductor and the
        volume pot. Use the exact manufacturer part number given.

    "EQUIVALENT OK - must meet spec"   (47 lines)
        Generic passives. Any manufacturer is fine PROVIDED the part
        meets the full spec in the Description column - value,
        tolerance, dielectric, voltage rating and package.

  Two things that are NOT negotiable within the "equivalent OK" group:
    - The 0.1 % thin-film resistors (R18-R37, 68k/33k/10k/90.9k).
      These set the preamp gain. 1 % parts are NOT acceptable.
    - C0G/NP0 dielectric where the BOM says C0G/NP0. Do not swap to
      X7R or X5R - these are in the audio and clock paths.
    - C1 and C66 electrolytics must be LOW-ESR types (switching
      regulator input/output).

  ONE known stock issue: SW3, the master gain encoder, MPN
  EC11E15244G1, was showing zero stock at Mouser. Any EC11E-family
  rotary encoder with the same footprint and shaft is acceptable.
