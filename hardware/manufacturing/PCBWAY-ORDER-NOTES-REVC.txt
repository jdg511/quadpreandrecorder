PASTE INTO "Detailed information of assembly"
-------------------------------------------------------------
Two-sided assembly, 293 placements (180 top, 113 bottom), mixed SMT
and through-hole. BOM and centroid files: QuadPreRecorder-BOM.csv and
QuadPreRecorder-CPL-PCBWay.csv (KiCad coordinate convention, Y negative
= downward). DNP: R5, R38-R41 (listed in BOM as DNP, not in CPL).

U8 (BOTTOM side) is a socketed sub-assembly - see
QuadPreRecorder-BOM-extras.csv: solder TWO Samtec SLW-124-01-G-S
low-profile 1x24 female socket strips into the U8 footprint (the two
24-pin rows, 15.24 mm apart; corrected 2026-09-15, the earlier package had them 17.78 mm apart), then plug a PJRC TEENSY41_PINS (Teensy
4.1 with pins pre-soldered) into the sockets with its microSD slot
toward the board edge marked "SD CARD SLOT THIS EDGE". BEFORE plugging
it in: (a) cut the small VIN-VUSB link pad on the Teensy's underside,
(b) solder two ~20 mm wires from the Teensy's underside "USB Device"
D+ and D- pads to the board's bottom-side pads TP1 (D+) and TP2 (D-)
next to the socket. If you cannot source PJRC parts, quote the
sockets only and we will supply and wire the Teensy ourselves.
TEENSY41_NE_PINS (no Ethernet chip) is acceptable.

J3 is a 1x14 2.54 mm FEMALE socket (Sullins PPTC141LFBN-RC) - do NOT
solder a display to it. We would like you to quote and ship LOOSE
(not fitted) the off-board items in QuadPreRecorder-BOM-extras.csv:
LCDWiki MSP3526 3.5in CAPACITIVE-touch IPS TFT module (not the
resistive MSP3520), 4x M3x11 standoffs + screws, a 9 VDC 1 A regulated
adapter with 5.5x2.1 mm CENTER-POSITIVE plug (US), a USB-C cable, one
push-on knob for a 6 mm 18-spline knurled shaft, and one Omron B32
keycap for the B3F-5150. If any of these are unavailable, quote the
rest and we will supply them.

J9 is an HRO TYPE-C-31-M-12 USB-C receptacle (16-pin, through-hole
shell tabs) - equivalent 16-pin TYPE-C-31-M-12 style parts are fine.
K1 is an Omron G6K-2F-Y DC5 signal relay - please do not substitute a
different pinout. J10 is a JST B2B-PH-K-S battery connector on the
BOTTOM side; the LiPo battery itself is customer-supplied (do not
fit). U13 (BQ24074) and U10 (TPA6130A2) have exposed pads - please reflow, not hand-solder; U10's pad has a 3x3 via array
(tent the bottom). C17-C20, C25-C28, C101-C104 (Rubycon 35MU475KC44532) and C57/C58/C86/C87 (Rubycon 25MU225MB23225) are PMLCAP
film chips, NOT ceramics: do not substitute MLCCs. They are cheapest from LCSC (C3778321 / C3778570); the 16 V siblings
16MU475MC14532 / 16MU225MB23225 are acceptable alternates. Please do not
substitute the electret ESD diodes with high-capacitance TVS parts,
and do not substitute the Neutrik, Alps, CUI or Amphenol connectors/
controls with look-alikes ("accept substitutes" = NO for those);
generic R/C substitutes of equal value/package/rating are fine.
Keep each four-resistor 0.1% set (matched channel resistors) from one
lot. Verify pin-1 orientation of U1, U4-U7, U9-U12 and the relay K1
polarity (coil pin 1 = +) against the assembly PDFs
(QuadPreRecorder-assembly-top.pdf / -bottom.pdf).
Fiducials: three 1 mm fiducials per side (not in BOM/CPL).

Sourcing notes (checked 2026-09-12, see BOM-SOURCING-CHECK-20260912.md
and QuadPreRecorder-best-source-20260912.csv for the cheapest in-stock
source of every line): every MPN in this BOM was in stock at Mouser
and/or LCSC on that date, none is a reel-only or end-of-life number.
U12 is now the Analog Devices ADP7142AUJZ-R7 (TSOT-23-5; Mouser 11k,
LCSC C514356) - the old TPS7A4701 is gone from this revision. Please buy
the Rubycon PMLCAP film caps, the Yageo 0.1 % resistors and the
TPS7A2033 / AO3401A / USB-C parts from LCSC (C3778321, C3778570, C705768,
C2862740, C15127, C165948 etc.); the ICs U7, U9, U11, U13 and the OPA1654s
are also cheaper at LCSC than at Mouser. For the C0G lines (C29-C32
10 nF, C67/C68 2.2 nF, 22 pF lines) any substitute must also be C0G/NP0.
D4/D5 (Kingbright 3 mm LEDs) may be swapped for any blue / red LED in
the same footprint.
