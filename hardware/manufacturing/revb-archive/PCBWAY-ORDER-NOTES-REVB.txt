and through-hole. BOM and centroid files: QuadPreRecorder-BOM.csv and

U8 (BOTTOM side) is a socketed sub-assembly - see
QuadPreRecorder-BOM-extras.csv: solder TWO Samtec SLW-124-01-G-S
low-profile 1x24 female socket strips into the U8 footprint (the two
24-pin rows, 17.78 mm apart), then plug a PJRC TEENSY41_PINS (Teensy
4.1 with pins pre-soldered) into the sockets with its microSD slot
toward the board edge marked "SD CARD SLOT THIS EDGE". If you cannot
source PJRC parts, quote the sockets only and we will supply the
Teensy. TEENSY41_NE_PINS (no Ethernet chip) is acceptable.

J3 is a bare 1x14 2.54 mm header - do NOT solder a display to it. We
would like you to quote and ship LOOSE (not fitted) the off-board
items in QuadPreRecorder-BOM-extras.csv: LCDWiki MSP3520 3.5in
capacitive-touch TFT module, 4x M3x10 standoffs + screws, a 9 VDC 1 A
regulated adapter with 5.5x2.1 mm CENTER-POSITIVE plug (US), two
push-on knobs for 6 mm 18-spline knurled shafts, and one Omron B32
keycap for the B3F-5150. If any of these are unavailable, quote the
rest and we will supply them.

J6/J7 are 2x13 2.54 mm pin headers. Please do not substitute the
electret ESD diodes with high-capacitance TVS parts, and do not
