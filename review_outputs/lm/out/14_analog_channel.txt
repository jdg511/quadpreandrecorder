BLOCKER: The full-scale check in Section 9 contains a critical arithmetic error. It states the preamp clips at "9 V rail / 2 / 10 = 0.3 Vrms". This calculation is incorrect; 9/2/10 is 0.45 V, not 0.3 V. More importantly, the conclusion that the ADC clips first is flawed because it ignores the actual op-amp output swing limit. With a 9V single supply and 4.5V reference, the max output swing is roughly 3.9Vrms (as correctly noted later in the text). The ADC full scale is 2.1Vrms. Since 3.9Vrms > 2.1Vrms, the ADC *does* clip first, but the intermediate calculation "9 V rail / 2 / 10 = 0.3 Vrms" is mathematically wrong and confusing. The correct input level for ADC full scale is 2.1Vrms / 10.09 gain = 0.208Vrms. The text's "0.3 Vrms" figure is unsupported by the math shown.

SHOULD FIX: In the "Mic-mode logic" table, the "Electret 5 V" row lists +9V_MIC as "~4.7 V (D24 drop)". However, the "Boot / unknown" and "Dynamic / balanced" rows list +9V_MIC as "0 V (bleeds through R104)". If Q8 is ON for 5V mode, the rail is pulled to ~4.7V. If Q8 is OFF (as in boot/dynamic), the rail is 0V. This is consistent. However, the sequencing rule says "drop MIC9/MIC5 first... then raise COLD_CTRL". In "Dynamic / balanced" mode, COLD_CTRL is HIGH, which releases the cold leg into A1. But the table says "Pad allowed: NO (firmware lockout)". This is a firmware constraint, not a hardware one. The hardware allows the pad, but firmware prevents it. This is acceptable but should be clearly documented as a firmware requirement, not a hardware limitation. The risk is if firmware fails, the pad could be active in dynamic mode, causing incorrect gain. This is a SHOULD FIX for documentation clarity.
