SHOULD FIX: D14 B340A is listed on BAT_SYS (pin 2) and VBOOST_IN (pin 1). B340A is a Schottky diode with anode at pin 1 and cathode at pin 2. If pin 1 is VBOOST_IN (higher voltage) and pin 2 is BAT_SYS (lower voltage), the diode is reverse-biased and will not conduct. The intent is likely to OR the battery into the boost input, so the anode should be BAT_SYS and cathode VBOOST_IN. As drawn, the battery cannot power the boost converter.

SHOULD FIX: D1 B340A is listed on +9V_FUSED (implied by tree) and VBOOST_IN (pin 1). The tree shows D1 cathode going to VBOOST_IN. If D1 anode is +9V_FUSED and cathode is VBOOST_IN, this is correct for OR-ing. However, the rail membership lists D1.1 on VBOOST_IN. If pin 1 is anode, it should be on +9V_FUSED. If pin 1 is cathode, it should be on VBOOST_IN. Standard B340A pinout: 1=Anode, 2=Cathode. If D1.1 (Anode) is on VBOOST_IN, the diode is reverse-biased. It should be D1.2 (Cathode) on VBOOST_IN. Check pin assignment.
